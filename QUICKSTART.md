# Quickstart (for pilot participants)

You do not need to read anything else in this repository to take part.

1. Ask the facilitator to run `npm start`, then open the link they give you.
2. Click **"What is this?"** at the top once — it's four sentences.
3. In **Setup**, pick the case and your role (Analyst A, Analyst B, or Reviewer). If you're
   unsure, ask the facilitator.
4. If you're an analyst: go to **Analyst Entry**. Choose whichever entry mode feels more
   natural — writing normal prose first is fine, the tool will help you structure it
   afterward, and nothing is used until you personally confirm it.
5. If you're the reviewer: go to **Reviewer Matching** once both analysts have saved a path.
   Confirm, reject, or add matches. Only check "reviewer-confirmed contradiction" if the two
   statements genuinely cannot both be true.
6. Click **Generate report**, then look at **Results**.
7. Grade what you see using the one-page rubric the facilitator gives you
   (`docs/SCORING_RUBRIC.md`).

That's it. If something breaks or is confusing, say so out loud — confusion is data.
